About

The story so far

I go by FirstOrbit online — an indie game developer who's been building interactive experiences since age 16. My first ever finished game was BioTech, and I haven't stopped since.

I've shipped games through game jams, built idle clickers in a single night, and had one of my games featured in a YouTube video. The creative process — the iteration, the failing, the shipping — is what drives me.

Beyond games, I've always expressed myself through drawing, storytelling, and music. Creativity isn't a side hobby; it's how I make sense of the world.
